Road Damage on Society and Economy

About Our Project

This project explains how damaged roads affect society, transport, public safety, and the economy.

Problems Faced by People

  • Road accidents
  • Traffic issues
  • Vehicle damage
  • Fuel waste
  • Delays in emergency services

Survey Results

80% people said roads cause accidents.

65% people said vehicle maintenance costs increased.
